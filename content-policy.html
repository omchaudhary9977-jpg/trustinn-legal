Content Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

This policy applies to everything you post or send through TrustInn: profile photos, descriptions, reviews, certificates, messages and booking details. It keeps the platform honest and safe for Customers and Partners.

1. What is not allowed

  • Fake, paid or misleading reviews and ratings, or reviews of a job that did not happen.
  • Photos or documents that are not yours, are edited to mislead, or show another person.
  • False identity, false certificates or false claims about skills, experience or verification.
  • Abuse, threats, harassment, hate or discrimination based on religion, caste, gender, region or anything else.
  • Sexual, violent or otherwise offensive content.
  • Illegal services or goods, or a request to do anything unlawful or unsafe.
  • Spam, advertising other apps or services, or sharing someone else's private information.
  • Asking users to take bookings or payments off the platform in order to avoid its rules.

2. Profile photos

Partners must use a clear, recent photo of their own face. No logos, group photos, filters that hide the face, or photos of other people.

3. Reviews

  • Write about your own experience, honestly and politely.
  • Do not include phone numbers, addresses or personal details.
  • A Partner may not review themselves or ask others to post false reviews.

4. Reporting

If you see content that breaks this policy, email trustinnsupport@gmail.com with a short description and, if possible, a screenshot.

5. What we may do

We may remove content, hide a review, warn a user, suspend or permanently remove an account, and where the law requires, report the matter to the authorities. We decide based on how serious the breach is and whether it has happened before.
