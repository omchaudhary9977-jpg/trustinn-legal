Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

TrustInn is an app operated by RedBirdz ("we", "us"). It connects customers with local service professionals ("Partners"). This policy explains what we collect, why, who sees it, and the choices you have. By using the App you accept this policy.

1. Information we collect

Information you give us:

  • Account details: name, phone number (verified by OTP), and optionally an email address.
  • Customer details: city, state, area and address you enter for bookings.
  • Partner details: profile photo, service category and skills, service area, and an optional "About" text.
  • Verification documents (Partners): a government ID and a selfie, used only to confirm identity.
  • Certificates (Partners, optional): trade certificates you choose to upload.
  • Bookings: service requests, job details, schedule, and messages linked to a booking.
  • Reviews and ratings you leave after a job.
  • Merchandise orders (Partners): the details you give when ordering.

Information collected automatically:

  • Location: your city and area, and with your permission your device location, to match you with nearby Partners.
  • Device and notification data: a device token used to send booking updates.
  • Basic usage data needed to run the App, such as app version and bookings made.

We do not access your photo gallery. When you choose a photo, Android shows its own picker and we receive only the image you select.

2. How we use it

  • Create and manage your account.
  • Connect Customers with suitable Partners.
  • Verify a Partner's identity before they accept jobs.
  • Send booking notifications and verification results.
  • Keep the platform safe, handle reports and enforce our policies.
  • Improve the App and fix technical problems.

3. ID documents

  • They are stored privately and are never publicly accessible.
  • Only authorised administrators can view one, through a temporary link that expires within minutes.
  • Each time a document is viewed or deleted, we log it.
  • We use ID documents only for identity verification and do not share them, except where the law requires.
  • A document can be permanently deleted once it is no longer needed for verification.

4. Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only:

  • Between Customer and Partner: to complete a booking they can see each other's name, phone number and job details.
  • With service providers that run the App: Firebase (Google) for phone sign-in and push notifications, and Supabase for the database and secure file storage.
  • When the law requires, or to protect the rights and safety of our users and the public.

5. Payments

TrustInn does not process or store any payment for services. Customers and Partners settle payment between themselves outside the App. We do not collect card or bank details.

6. How long we keep data

We keep account and booking information while your account is active and as long as needed to meet legal obligations, resolve disputes and enforce our agreements. When you delete your account, your personal details are removed or anonymised. Some booking and review records may be kept in anonymised form so that other users' histories stay accurate.

7. Your rights and choices

  • Access, correct or delete your information by writing to trustinnsupport@gmail.com.
  • Delete your account yourself in the App: Profile > Delete account. If you can no longer open the App, email us and we will delete it for you.
  • Withdraw location permission at any time in your device settings. Some features may then not work.
  • Turn off notifications in your device settings.

8. Children

TrustInn is only for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from minors.

9. Security

We use access controls, authentication and time-limited links for sensitive files. No system is completely secure, so we cannot promise absolute security.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. We will change the "Last updated" date and, for important changes, tell you in the App.

11. Contact
