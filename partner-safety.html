Partner Safety & Responsibility

Last updated: November 3, 2026

This notice is for every Partner on TrustInn. Please read it carefully. By registering as a Partner you confirm that you have read and accepted it.

1. You are an independent professional

Partners work for themselves. You are not an employee, agent or contractor of TrustInn or RedBirdz. You choose which jobs to accept, and you decide how to do the work.

2. Responsibility for injury and accidents

You do your work at your own risk. If you are injured, fall ill, have an accident or suffer any loss while on duty, travelling to or from a job, or at a customer's location, TrustInn and RedBirdz are not responsible and do not provide compensation, medical cover or insurance. You are responsible for your own safety, your tools and equipment, and for any damage or injury you cause to others or to property.

We strongly recommend that every Partner arranges personal accident and health insurance.

3. Safety tips

  • Use proper protective gear for the job: gloves, helmet, safety shoes, goggles, mask.
  • Electrical work: switch off the mains and use a tester before touching any wire. Never work on live wiring or with wet hands.
  • Heights: use a stable ladder with a helper. Do not lean out or stand on the top step. Do not work at height in rain, wind or alone on risky jobs.
  • Solar and rooftop work: use a harness where needed, avoid roofs that are wet or weak, and do not work in strong sun without water and shade.
  • Chemicals and pesticides: read the label, wear a mask and gloves, keep the area ventilated, and keep children and pets away.
  • Tools and machines: check them before use, unplug before changing parts, and never remove safety guards.
  • Vehicle work: park on level ground, apply the brake, and keep away from moving parts and hot exhausts.
  • Do not take on a job that you are not trained for or that feels unsafe. Say no and cancel politely.
  • Tell someone you trust where you are going, and keep your phone charged.
  • Take breaks and drink water, especially in heat.

4. If something goes wrong

  • Stop work and move to a safe place.
  • Call 112 for emergencies, or 108 for an ambulance. For fire, call 101.
  • Inform the Customer and, when you can, email trustinnsupport@gmail.com.

5. Customers' part

Customers are expected to provide a safe place to work, clear access, and accurate information about the job. A Partner may refuse or stop any job that is unsafe.

6. Acceptance

By registering as a Partner you agree that you understand this notice and that you accept the responsibilities in it.
