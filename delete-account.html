Delete your TrustInn account

TrustInn is a local-services marketplace by RedBirdz. You can delete your account and personal data at any time, either inside the app or by sending us a request.

Option 1 — Delete inside the app (fastest)

  1. Open the TrustInn app and log in.
  2. Go to Profile.
  3. Tap Delete account, type DELETE to confirm.

Your account is deleted straight away.

Option 2 — Request deletion by email

If you can no longer open the app, email us at trustinnsupport@gmail.com from your registered email, or tell us the phone number registered on your account. Subject: Delete my TrustInn account. We will confirm and delete your account within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your name, phone number, email, profile photo and address details
  • For partners: your ID document, certificates, profile description and verification details
  • Your notifications and saved partners
  • Your login — you will no longer be able to sign in with that number

What may be kept

  • Completed bookings, reviews and merchandise orders are kept without your personal details, because they are also part of the other person's records and our accounting. They no longer identify you.
  • Records we are required by law to keep (for example, payment and tax records), for the period the law requires.

Before you delete

You cannot delete your account while you have a booking in progress. Complete or cancel it first.
